    What is SDA?

    Housing that's
    funded for you.

    Specialist Disability Accommodation (SDA) is housing funded through Australia's NDIS for people with extreme functional impairment or very high support needs.

    That means your rent is covered through your NDIS plan — so you can focus on living well, not on housing costs.

    Learn more at NDIS.gov.au

    How does SDA funding work?

    SDA funding is paid directly to the landlord through your NDIS plan. Eligible participants can live in purpose-built accessible housing without paying standard rental costs out of pocket.

    Am I eligible?

    SDA is for NDIS participants with extreme functional impairment or very high support needs. Your support coordinator or NDIS planner can assess your eligibility and help you apply.

    How do I apply?

    Contact ADAPT Housing (SDA provider) about availability, or Challenge Community Services (SIL provider) about care and support arrangements. Reach out to us at Brookline and we'll connect you with the right people.
